How Solar Energy Works

From sunlight to electricity in four simple steps.

01

Sunlight

Sunlight reaches the surface of solar panels.

02

Solar Cells

Photovoltaic cells absorb sunlight and generate electrical energy.

03

Inverter

An inverter converts the electricity into a form suitable for normal use.

04

Power

The electricity can power appliances and buildings.

Solar technology can be used at different scales.

Rooftop Solar

Panels installed on rooftops can generate electricity for homes, schools and offices.

Solar Farms

Large solar installations use many panels together to produce electricity at scale.

Commercial Solar

Businesses can use solar systems to generate electricity for their buildings and operations.

Why Choose Solar Energy?

Solar power can offer environmental and practical advantages.

☀️

Renewable

🌿

Cleaner Energy

💰

🔋

Solar systems can also work with batteries to store electricity for later use.

Solar Energy in Everyday Life

Solar energy is becoming useful in many parts of everyday life. It can provide clean electricity, reduce dependence on conventional power sources and support a more sustainable lifestyle.

Homes

Solar panels can be installed on rooftops to generate electricity for lights, fans, appliances and other household needs. Home solar systems can also be combined with batteries for storing electricity.

Schools & Offices

Schools, offices and commercial buildings can use solar power for daily electricity requirements. Using rooftop solar can help these buildings make better use of available space while producing renewable electricity.

Solar Technology

Solar energy is also used in street lights, water heating, calculators, outdoor lighting and other useful technologies. These applications show how sunlight can be converted into practical energy for everyday activities.
